# Azzambfit roadmap

What we build next for lifestyle clients, ordered by what a normal person needs to keep going.

## Rules of this board

1. The client is a lifestyle client. If an item does not help a normal person show up next week, it is parked with a reason or it stays off the board.
2. Every item names the loop step it fixes and the evidence it stands on. Evidence is one of production, demand elsewhere, the adherence PRD, a device check, or a product call.
3. Now stays small. Capacity is fixed, so parking is the discipline — MacroFactor's board carries 48 open asks and 113,863 votes, four years unresolved. That is the failure mode.
4. Advanced and heavy-user scope is parked and hidden behind the lens, so it can never quietly become the plan.
5. Order is decided in the repo, not on the screen. Edit src/items.ts and rebuild — the first item in a column is the next one to pick up.

Parked reasons:

- **Client web app** — Our clients are phone-first. Revisit if desktop signup shows real demand.
- **Alcohol calorie calculator** — Not our market. Choosing our users means saying no.
- **Micronutrient targets (iodine, %DV, pregnancy)** — Advanced scope. Energy, protein, fat, carbs and fibre cover the lifestyle job.
- **Net carbs** — Serves one diet tribe and confuses everyone else at the label.
- **Garmin, Oura and Whoop integrations** — Apple Health covers the realistic device mix here; integrations multiply support cost.
- **Digestive tracker** — Weak loop fit. Revisit only if gut complaints recur in check-in reasons.
- **Apple Watch companion** — A watch app serves heavy users. Phone-first until the adherence data says otherwise.
- **Family and household plan** — A commercial decision, and there is no payment layer yet. After the loop proves itself.
